Our route this summer takes us across four countries. We start in Lisbon, follow the coast up through Porto, then cross the border into Spain and spend a few days in Madrid and Barcelona. From there we drive along the Mediterranean into France, stopping in Marseille and Lyon before reaching Paris. If there is time, we will take the train under the Channel to London and finish the trip with a walk along the Thames. I have wanted to see the Pyrenees for years, and the Alps are supposed to be breathtaking in the late afternoon light. Wherever we end up, I am bringing a proper camera this time.
