The path narrowed as it climbed, and the trees closed in overhead until the sky was only a scatter of bright coins between the leaves. Everything smelled of damp earth and pine. Somewhere off to the left a stream muttered over stones, hidden but constant, and now and then a bird broke the quiet with a single sharp note. I stopped at a clearing to catch my breath and realized I had not heard a car, a phone, or another human voice in over an hour. The silence was not empty; it was full of small sounds you never notice until the louder ones fall away. I stayed there a long while, reluctant to move on.
