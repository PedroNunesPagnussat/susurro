This is the easiest bread you will ever make, and it needs only four ingredients. Mix the flour, water, salt, and yeast in a large bowl until there are no dry patches, then cover it and forget about it overnight. In the morning the dough should be bubbly and loose. Tip it onto a floured surface, fold it over on itself a few times, and shape it into a rough ball. Heat your oven as hot as it will go, with a heavy pot inside. Drop the dough into the hot pot, cover it, and bake it until the crust is deep golden brown. Let it cool before you cut it, if you can resist.
